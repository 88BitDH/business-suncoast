BUSINESS TERMS OF SERVICE

Suncoast Tech Support · Operated by 88 Bit Diamond Hands LLC

Draft revision prepared November 7, 2026. Subject to business and legal approval before publication.

These Terms are intended for organizations that purchase business IT support, managed services, field services, cybersecurity assistance, consulting, or related business services from Suncoast Tech Support ("Suncoast," "we," "us"). Residential consumer purchases are covered by separate Residential Terms. A written master service agreement (MSA), statement of work (SOW), purchase order accepted by Suncoast, partner contract, or government contract may provide different requirements; where documents conflict, the specific negotiated and signed agreement controls.

1. Parties, order approval and scope

The customer ("Client") must have authority to request the services and authorize access to the relevant systems. Work is governed by the accepted scope, deliverables, schedule and service levels in a written proposal, quote, ticket approval, SOW or similar confirmation. A request outside the approved scope requires notice and additional Client approval before any additional charge. We do not promise specific service levels, round-the-clock support or a particular outcome unless agreed in writing.

2. Services and third-party products

Services may include onsite or remote troubleshooting, endpoint administration, networking, Microsoft 365 and other third-party licensing, data migration, configuration, backup assistance, cybersecurity review, project consulting and MSP field dispatch. Third-party software, connectivity, hardware, hosted services and subscriptions are governed by their own applicable licenses, warranties, service levels and privacy terms. Availability, feature changes, vendor outages and external licensing fees are outside Suncoast's direct control. Any reseller commitment or required license minimum will be disclosed in the approved order.

3. Client responsibilities and authorization

Client represents that it owns or has legal authority over the accounts, devices, networks, facilities and information for which it requests service. Client will provide authorized credentials, required licenses, safe onsite access, relevant documentation, timely decisions and a point of contact. Client is responsible for keeping appropriate independent backups and approving any operation that may erase, overwrite or move material data. Suncoast will request authorization before significant or potentially destructive changes.

4. Security, confidentiality and regulated data

Each party will take reasonable care to protect confidential information received while performing its duties. Suncoast will use and disclose Client information only as needed to provide authorized services, use approved service providers, respond to lawful requests or meet agreed obligations. Security measures, privileged account access, incident notifications, regulated-data processing, retention standards, and compliance responsibilities will be defined in a signed SOW, security addendum or data-processing agreement when necessary. We do not represent that a standard troubleshooting appointment alone provides a comprehensive security assessment, HIPAA compliance, tax data safeguards compliance, legal preservation or ongoing monitoring.

5. Fees, invoicing, changes and taxes

Rates, minimum billing, expenses, materials, applicable deposits, included support time and payment schedule will be specified in a written quote, invoice, or accepted order. Additional labor, travel beyond an agreed service area, emergency or after-hours work, additional users or devices, and third-party fees require prior approval where outside scope. Unless the invoice or contract specifies other terms, payment is due when the invoice states. Applicable taxes will be calculated and charged when legally required based on the nature of the products and services. No blanket exemption is promised for all technical labor, subscriptions or software.

For recurring business service plans, the order must identify price, billing interval, initial term, renewal/cancellation procedure, included services and any third-party vendor commitment that continues beyond a cancellation. Suncoast will provide clear cancellation instructions and an accurate final invoice.

6. Deposits, scheduling, rescheduling and cancellations

A deposit may be requested before scheduling work or ordering hardware. The invoice or approved order will state whether it is refundable, how it is applied, and any actual third-party noncancelable commitments. We will not assume that all deposits are always nonrefundable merely because an appointment was scheduled. Cancellation or rescheduling charges, if any, must be disclosed before the customer agrees and must be lawful and reasonably related to the agreed terms.

7. Failed payments and service suspension

Suncoast may notify Client of an overdue balance and suspend new nonessential services if payment remains overdue, subject to the order, applicable law, and any agreed notice and transition obligations. Reasonable late fees or collection costs apply only if disclosed in the contract or invoice and legally permitted. Client's lawful access to its own data and account credentials is not forfeited solely because an invoice is disputed or outstanding. We will cooperate in a reasonable transition and may invoice separately agreed transition labor.

8. Standard of care and limits of technology work

Suncoast will perform services with reasonable care and skill consistent with the agreed scope. Outcomes may depend on equipment condition, backup integrity, user actions, third-party systems, software compatibility, network design, and external threats. We cannot guarantee complete data preservation, prevention of every security incident, permanent elimination of malware, uninterrupted internet availability or specific hardware performance unless a written warranty explicitly provides otherwise. Clients should keep verified backups and promptly report problems.

9. Responsibility and limitation of liability

Subject to the specific negotiated MSA or SOW and to the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, each party will be responsible for losses directly caused by its own breach or legally actionable conduct. Neither party excludes liability where the law prohibits it. Any proposed limitation on indirect, consequential or special damages, or any dollar cap on direct damages, must be agreed clearly in an applicable written service agreement. Absent such agreement, legal rights and remedies remain governed by applicable law. This section does not eliminate duties of confidentiality, authorized data handling, or the duty to exercise reasonable care.

10. Indemnification

Neither party agrees to an unlimited or automatic indemnity merely because a third party makes a claim. Any duty to defend, indemnify or reimburse claims, including its scope, procedure, exclusions and liability limits, must be expressly described in a signed MSA, SOW or other approved written agreement. Client remains responsible for securing appropriate permissions for its systems and content.

11. Payment disputes and chargebacks

Client should notify Suncoast promptly of billing errors or disputed work so the parties can review invoices, service records and agreed scope. Clients retain lawful rights to raise payment disputes with their bank or card issuer, and Suncoast retains lawful rights to respond with records showing services and authorization. Neither party may impose a blanket prohibition on statutory payment disputes. Any fee related to a dispute must be legally permissible and expressly agreed where required.

12. Customer property, data return and equipment disposal

Client-owned equipment and information remain Client property. Unless otherwise agreed, Suncoast will return Client-provided hardware and make an orderly handoff of account access, configurations and deliverables reasonably required by the scope. Hardware recycling, secure wiping, chain-of-custody procedures and destruction certification require a specific approved service description; a general disposal request does not imply a certified destruction service. For migrations, access handoff and backup work, Client should independently verify final data and account accessibility.

13. Dispute resolution and governing law

Both parties agree to first attempt a good-faith resolution by exchanging a written description of the issue and supporting records. They may voluntarily agree to mediation before litigation. Mandatory mediation, arbitration, exclusive venue, fee shifting or other restrictions apply only where specifically accepted in a separately negotiated agreement and enforceable under applicable law. In the absence of a controlling written agreement, disputes are handled under applicable law; Florida law may govern services delivered in Florida, subject to any mandatory rules.

14. Ordering priority, changes and severability

For a particular engagement, the order of priority is: (1) mandatory law and controlling public-sector requirements, (2) executed MSA, subcontract or other negotiated agreement, (3) accepted SOW or quote, and (4) these website Terms. Changes to fees, services or contractual duties require notice and acceptance where required. If any provision is invalid, remaining provisions apply to the extent lawful. Confidentiality, payment obligations for authorized services, and valid post-termination responsibilities survive only as reasonably necessary or as set out in the controlling agreement.
