<main><div class="alerts-hero"><div class="alerts-wrap"><a class="alerts-back" href="/alerts/">← All Alerts &amp; Insights</a><span class="alerts-eyebrow">SECURITY GUIDANCE · PATCH MANAGEMENT</span><h1>Windows and MSP management security: what your office should verify</h1><p>Business computers and management systems need a clear update process, especially when CISA reports active exploitation.</p></div></div><article class="alerts-article"><div class="alerts-wrap alerts-article-inner"><p class="alerts-byline">By Suncoast Tech Support · Published December 1, 2026 · Small-business cybersecurity</p><p>In its September 8, 2026 advisory, CISA added four vulnerabilities to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, including <strong>CVE-2026-81963</strong> and <strong>CVE-2026-85880</strong> affecting Microsoft Windows, and <strong>CVE-2026-86218</strong> affecting N-able N-central. Another listed issue concerned Adobe Commerce and Magento.</p>
<p>The bulletin applies to specific products and vulnerable versions, not every Windows computer or MSP management system. The enduring lesson for a small office is to know which equipment and management tools it relies on—and whether updates and access reviews are actually happening.</p>
<h2>Five-point update and access checklist</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>List your business devices.</strong> Record Windows PCs, laptops, servers and their installed operating-system versions, including machines used remotely.</li>
<li><strong>Check patch status.</strong> Use approved Microsoft and vendor instructions to confirm applicable security updates have been installed and restarted where necessary.</li>
<li><strong>Ask about management platforms.</strong> If an IT provider uses N-able N-central or other remote-management software, confirm vendor patches and administrative security are kept current.</li>
<li><strong>Limit powerful access.</strong> Use separate administrative accounts where appropriate, enforce MFA and remove stale technician access.</li>
<li><strong>Maintain a recovery plan.</strong> Verify endpoint backups, recovery contacts and how the office will respond to unexpected changes or suspected compromise.</li>
</ol>
<div class="alerts-caution"><p><strong>Not all devices are affected:</strong> CISA's catalog prioritizes specific vulnerabilities with evidence of exploitation. Verify the affected version, exposure, patch and operational risk before assuming a system is compromised.</p></div>
