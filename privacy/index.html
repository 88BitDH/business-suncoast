1. Scope and Purpose

This Privacy Policy governs how 88-Bit Diamond Hands, LLC, operating as Suncoast Tech Support (“Company”), collects, accesses, uses, stores, and discloses information in the course of providing information technology services.

By requesting or authorizing services, the client (“Client”) acknowledges and agrees to the data practices described herein.

2. Types of Information Accessed

In the course of providing services, the Company may access, view, handle, transmit, or store, intentionally or incidentally, the following types of information:

  • Personal information (names, email addresses, phone numbers)
  • Business information
  • System files and operating system data
  • Documents, photos, emails, and media files

  • Financial or confidential records stored on Client devices
  • Network traffic and configuration data

The Company does not determine the nature or sensitivity of data stored on Client systems and does not audit or classify Client data.

3. Client Responsibility for Confidential and Sensitive Data

The Client is solely responsible for:

The Company does not assume responsibility for identifying, segregating, encrypting, or protecting confidential or regulated data, including but not limited to:

  • Medical or health information (HIPAA)
  • Financial or banking data
  • Legal or attorney-client privileged material
  • Trade secrets or intellectual property
  • Personally Identifiable Information (PII)

4. No Confidentiality or Fiduciary Relationship

Unless expressly agreed to in a separate written agreement signed by both parties:

  • No attorney-client, accountant-client, or confidential advisory relationship is created

Access to Client data is incidental to providing technical services only.

5. Data Use Limitation

Any access to Client data is performed solely for the purpose of delivering requested services, troubleshooting, testing, or verifying functionality.

The Company does not intentionally review, use, disclose, or exploit Client data beyond what is reasonably necessary to perform services.
