Frequently asked questions

Common questions about business IT support and the first consultation.

Is the initial 15-minute consultation free?

Yes. The initial conversation is free. Troubleshooting, technical assessments, repairs and project work are separately quoted or billed.

Do I need a monthly support contract?

No. Suncoast offers one-time business support and projects as well as defined monthly support plans.

Can you help remotely or do you visit offices?

Many issues can be handled remotely. Onsite service is available for suitable work within the local service area, with travel terms confirmed in advance.

Which businesses do you support?

Suncoast works with small offices, professional practices and local businesses, including accounting and tax firms that need technical FTC Safeguards assistance.

What does one-time business support cost?

Remote business IT support is $125 per hour; onsite support is $159 per hour, each with a one-hour minimum. Travel, equipment, licenses and larger projects may cost extra.

Does your FTC Safeguards work guarantee compliance?

No. Suncoast provides technical assessments and implementation assistance, not legal advice or a regulatory compliance guarantee.
