Frequently asked questions

Who is this service for?

Tax preparation businesses and accounting offices seeking technical assistance with client-data safeguards. Whether the FTC Safeguards Rule applies to your particular activities is a separate business and legal determination.

Does Suncoast certify my business as FTC-compliant?

No. Suncoast can assess agreed technical safeguards, document findings, and implement scoped improvements. Regulatory compliance and any required program oversight remain the firm's responsibility.

Do I need a monthly contract?

No. You can begin with a one-time consultation or scoped assessment. Ongoing support or monitoring, if appropriate, is quoted separately.
